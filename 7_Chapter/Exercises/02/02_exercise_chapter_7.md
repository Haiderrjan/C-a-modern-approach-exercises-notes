### Exercise 7.02
Which of the following are not legal 
constants in C? Classify each legal 
constant as either integer or 
floating-point.

(a) 010E2

(b) 32.1E+5

(c) 0790

(d) 100_000

(e) 3.987e-2


### Answer:

**(a)** legal and is a floating constant

**(b)** legal and is a floating constant

**(c)** illegal as it is an octal and 9 is not 
a valid octal number.

**(d)**  illegal as underscores are invalid in C

**(e)** legal and it is a floating point number
