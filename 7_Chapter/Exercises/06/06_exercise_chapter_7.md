### Exercise 7.06

For each of the following items of data, 
specify which one of the types char, short, int,
or long is the smallest one guaranteed to be large 
enough to store the item.

**(a)**  Days in a month

**(b)**  Days in a year

**(c)**  Minutes in a day

**(d)**  Seconds in a day

### Answer:

**(a)** `char` - 31 days in a month

**(b)** `short` - 365 days in a year

**(c)** `short` - 1440 minutes in a day

**(d)**  `int` - 86400 seconds in a day
