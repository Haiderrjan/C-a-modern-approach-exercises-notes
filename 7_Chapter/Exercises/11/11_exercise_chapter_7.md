### Exercise 7.11
Suppose that `i` is a variable of type `int`, `f` is 
a variable of type `float`, and `d` is a variable 
of type `double`. What is the type of the 
expression `i * f / d?`



### Answer:

The expression becomes a `double` as `i` is a `int` which
converts to a `float` due to `f` then `f` turns into `double`
as `d` is a `double` due to the hierarchy.
