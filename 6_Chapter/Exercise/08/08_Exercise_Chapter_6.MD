### Exercise 6.08
 What output does the following for statement produce?
    
```
for (i = 10; i >= 1; i /= 2)
printf("%d ", i++);
```

Answer:

So the output will be `10 5 3 1` and then keep repeating one as `i` will 
always be greater and equal to `1` so the loop will keep on continuing due to the post-fix
increment in the printf statement.
