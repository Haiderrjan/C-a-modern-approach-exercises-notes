### Exercise 6.05
Which one of the following statements is not equivalent to the other two 
(assuming that the loop bodies are the same)?

```
(a) while (i < 10) {...}
(b) for (; i < 10;) {...}
(c) do {...} while (i < 10);

```

Answer:
The Third statement is not equivalent to the other statement as the loop runs first then
checks the condition after compared to the other which will always check the condition first.
